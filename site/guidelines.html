Community guidelines

Yap is for meeting people nearby in real life. These rules keep that safe and friendly for everyone.

Adults only

You must be 18 or older to use Yap. Report anyone who seems younger.

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or slurs, in messages, profiles, notes or events. Nobody is owed a chat: when someone says "not this time" or ends a chat, leave it there.

Keep it clean

No nudity or sexual content in photos, profiles, events or messages, and no asking for it.

Be yourself

Use your real first name and photos of you. No pretending to be someone else, fake accounts, spam, ads or selling.

Nothing illegal or dangerous

No violence, weapons, drugs, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.

Meet safely

Meet in public places, tell a friend where you are, and leave whenever you want to. Yap never shows anyone where you are.

If someone breaks the rules

Tap ··· on their profile or in your chat, or long-press their message in a group chat, to report or block them. Blocking is instant: you stop seeing each other. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours. Content that breaks these rules is removed, and people who break them are suspended or removed from Yap and can't sign up again with the same email. A profile reported by three different people who chatted with them on Yap, got a say-hi from them or saw them post in a shared group chat (or who have used Yap for a few days) is hidden straight away until it has been reviewed.

Slurs and abusive words are refused when you post them. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour.

In danger? Call your local emergency number first. Yap can't send help.
