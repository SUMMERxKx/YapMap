A little help with Yap.

Yap helps you meet people nearby, in person. Go live when you’re up for a chat, say hi, and meet in real life.

Need a hand?

Email support@samarxkx.dev for help with your account or a safety concern.

Joining the beta

Yap is an invite-only beta. It is open first to invited testers in Kamloops, BC, and other places, such as Vancouver, may follow later. On iPhone you install it through Apple’s TestFlight app, and on Android through a Google Play closed test, using the invitation we send you.

To ask for an invitation, email support@samarxkx.dev. During the beta, features can change or go away. You can send feedback through TestFlight or by email.

Signing in

Use the email address you signed up with. If you’ve forgotten your password, use the password reset option in Yap. If you’re still stuck, email support from your account’s email address. Never send your password or sign-in codes.

Staying safe

Tap ··· on a profile or in a chat, or long-press a group message, to report or block. You can also email support if you can’t use the app. Every report is reviewed within 24 hours.

Meet in public places, tell a friend where you are, and leave whenever you want. If you’re in danger, call your local emergency number first. Yap can’t send help.

Read the Community guidelines and Child Safety Standards.

Deleting your account

In Yap, open Profile → Settings → Delete account. Without the app, email support@samarxkx.dev from the address you use for Yap, with the subject “Delete my account”. We’ll verify the request and complete deletion within 30 days.

The account deletion page explains the steps, what is deleted and the safety records that can remain.
