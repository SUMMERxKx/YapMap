Delete your Yap account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Yap and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Tap Delete account. The screen lists what is deleted.
  4. Type your first name, as it is on your profile, to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

If your account is on hold (hidden after reports, or suspended), Yap opens on the "Your account is on hold" screen instead: tap Delete account there, then do step 4.

If you haven't finished setting up your profile, go back to the first setup screen (the one with "I'm 18 or older") and tap Delete account there. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@samarxkx.dev from the email address you use for Yap, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request came from you and delete the account within 30 days, the same way as in the app, then reply to let you know.

What gets deleted

Everything in your account is deleted from the live service, whether or not your account is on hold:

  • your account, email address and password
  • your profile and every photo you uploaded, including old and replaced ones
  • your live session, your last known area and the times you went live or moved
  • every say-hi request and 1:1 chat you were part of, with all their messages, for both of you
  • the events you host, with their members and group chats
  • your event memberships and every message you sent in other people's group chats
  • the people you blocked, and blocks other people made against you
  • any ban on your email address from a suspension, including one from another account with the same address (a ban from an account we removed for breaking the rules, or one we added by hand, stays)

Only safety reports made by you or about you are kept, without your account linked to them, so we can still act on them. Removing account links does not make report details anonymous: details, and the copy of the reported profile, message or event a report keeps (with up to the last 20 messages the reported person sent, for a report made from a say-hi or chat), may still identify you or someone else. Nothing else is kept in Yap. The TestFlight and Google Play tester lists are kept by Apple and Google, separate from Yap. Deleting your Yap account removes your Yap data but cannot remove you from those lists automatically. Email support@samarxkx.dev, or tell us when you leave the beta, and we remove your tester invite within 30 days.

The records of when you signed in are removed within 7 days, for everyone. Copies in our hosting provider's backups expire within 7 days (daily backups kept on the Supabase Pro plan). The safety reports described above can remain after those backups expire. See the Privacy Policy for the full retention details.
