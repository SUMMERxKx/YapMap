Delete your Yap account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Yap and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear (Settings).
  3. Tap Delete account. The screen lists what is deleted.
  4. Type your first name, as it is on your profile, to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

If your account is on hold (hidden after reports, or suspended), Yap opens on the "Your account is on hold" screen instead: tap Delete account there, then do step 4.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email support@samarxkx.dev from the email address you use for Yap, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm the request came from you and delete the account within 30 days, then reply to let you know.

What gets deleted

The following is deleted from the live service: your email address and password, your profile and all your photos, your live session, every say-hi request and chat you were part of and their messages, the events you host and their group chats, your event memberships and group messages, and the people you blocked.

Safety reports made by you or about you are kept, without your account linked to them, so we can still act on them. If your account was hidden or suspended when you deleted it, we keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email address so it can't be used to sign up again.

Removing account links does not make report details anonymous: details may still identify you or someone else. These safety records can remain after account deletion. Reports are kept so we can act if someone comes back; banned email hashes are kept until we lift the ban.

Copies in our hosting provider's backups expire within 7 days (daily backups kept on the Supabase Pro plan). The retained safety records described above can remain after those backups expire. See the Privacy Policy for the full retention details.
