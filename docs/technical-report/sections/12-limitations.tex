\chapter{Known limitations and future work}
\label{ch:limitations}

\Cref{tab:limitations} lists the known limitations of the current implementation and their effect.
\Cref{tab:planned} lists the work the project's open issues plan. None of the planned work is
implemented.

\begin{longtable}{@{}L{38mm}L{\dimexpr\linewidth-38mm-2\tabcolsep\relax}@{}}
  \caption{Known limitations.}
  \label{tab:limitations}\\
  \toprule
  \textsf{\textbf{Limitation}} & \textsf{\textbf{Effect and context}} \\
  \midrule
  \endfirsthead
  \toprule
  \textsf{\textbf{Limitation}} & \textsf{\textbf{Effect and context}} \\
  \midrule
  \endhead
  \bottomrule
  \endfoot
  \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textsf{\textbf{Product}}} \\
  No push notifications & Requests and messages arrive only while the app is open, so the screen stays on while live. A request to a locked or closed phone is shown only if the app returns within its five minutes; otherwise it expires as \ui{Not this time}. Push was deferred; the routes it would open already exist. \\
  One request and one chat at a time & By design. A request that arrives while another request or a chat is on screen is never shown and expires; its sender cannot tell it from a decline. \\
  No verification, social sign-in off & Selfie and face verification is not part of this release and profiles are not verified. Social sign-in is built behind a flag (\cref{sec:feature-flags}), and neither Apple nor Google is configured in Supabase Auth. \\
  Events cannot be edited & A host can delete an event but not change it. Events leave the map three hours after they start. \\
  Legal pages not yet published & The privacy policy and terms links open the addresses in \code{src/config/links.ts}, under \code{https://samarxkx.dev/yap}. The pages are drafts in \code{docs/legal/} that the owner must publish there before the first store release; at the time of writing those addresses are not live. \ui{Notification settings} opens the phone's settings although the app sends no notifications. \\
  Bounded lists & At most 50 people nearby, 50 events on the map and the newest 200 messages per chat. \\
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textsf{\textbf{Platform}}} \\
  No web target & The template's web settings remain (\code{app.json}, \code{npm run web}, \code{react-native-web}, a web branch in the Supabase client), but \code{react-native-maps} has no web implementation, so the web build is not supported. \\
  Foreground location only & Deliberate: the app never asks for background location, so it cannot notice a live user walking away; the session runs to its end or until the user goes offline. \\
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textsf{\textbf{Security and privacy}}} \\
  Relocation across sessions & A modified client can move its map position or a new session up to 5\,km plus 1000\,km/h from its last known position, up to three times in ten minutes, so it can claim to be somewhere it could have travelled to but is not (\cref{sec:trade-off}). \\
  Self-reported positions & The server stores only cells and limits how often and how fast a position changes, but it cannot verify the position the phone reports. A modified client can still send a fake position, within those limits, to see events or join one in another place; app attestation would be needed to close this. \\
  Signed URLs outlive visibility & A photo URL signed while its owner was visible works until it expires, up to fifteen minutes later, even after a block. \\
  Shared event chats & A block hides the blocked user's group messages from the blocker, but not the blocker's messages from the blocked user. \\
  Self-declared age & The 18+ confirmation is not verified. \\
  Manual moderation & Three counted reports hide a profile at once. The rest is done by hand in the Supabase SQL editor: reviewing reports, restoring a hidden profile with \code{private.restore_user}, removing content and suspending with \code{private.suspend_user}, as \code{docs/legal/moderation.md} describes. There is no moderator screen, and the daily review depends on the owner doing it. \\
  Cheap accounts & An account needs only an email address and a code. The sign-up hook refuses throwaway domains, banned addresses and more than 20 accounts a day from one IP address, and new accounts' reports cannot hide anyone on their own, but every other limit is per account; there is no CAPTCHA, social sign-in or device check yet. \\
  Narrow word filter & The filter refuses only whole words from a short list of slurs and abuse, also with look-alike digits. Other spellings, abuse without listed words and offensive photos get through; reports and blocks are the main defence. Names and report details are not filtered. \\
  Session storage & The session is kept in AsyncStorage, which is not encrypted, because it is too large for the platform's secure store. \\
  \midrule
  \multicolumn{2}{@{}l}{\textsf{\textbf{Operations}}} \\
  No retention policy & Requests, messages and past events are kept until an account is deleted. How long to keep chats is an open product decision. \\
  One environment & No staging project: development uses the hosted project, and a local stack serves only development of the database and behaviour proofs. \\
  No client tests & CI checks types, lint, project health, both release bundles, the migration files, a rebuild of the database from every migration, the SQL lint, the pgTAP tests and the generated types (\cref{sec:ci}), but the client has no unit or user-interface tests (\cref{ch:testing}). Branch protection that requires these checks is described in \code{docs/RELEASING.md} but not yet switched on. \\
\end{longtable}
