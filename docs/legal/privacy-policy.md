# Yap Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 20 November 2026

Yap is an app for meeting people who are nearby, in person. It is made by Samar Khajuria
("we", "us"). This policy explains what Yap keeps about you, why, who helps us run it, how
long it is kept and how to delete it.

Yap is an invite-only beta. It is open first to invited testers in Kamloops, British Columbia,
who install it through Apple's TestFlight or a Google Play closed test. "Beta testing" below
explains what that adds.

The short version: we keep what you put in your profile, the chats and events you take part
in, and your blocks, for as long as you have an account. When you delete your account, all of
it is deleted. Only safety reports are kept, without your account linked, as described below. We only ever store
your location as an area of about 100 metres, and keep only the last one, to check that the next
one is somewhere you could have travelled to. Nobody else sees your exact location or how far
away you are, but people live near you can tell that you are close, and a note you write can
say where you are. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps.

Questions or requests: **support@samarxkx.dev**.

## What we keep, and why

### Your account

- **Email address and password.** To create your account and sign you in. Your password is
  stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider; we can never read it.
- **The 8-digit codes we email you** to confirm your address or reset your password. They
  expire after a short time.
- **When you sign up,** we check your email address against a list of temporary-email services
  and against our list of banned addresses, and we keep a hashed copy of the IP address it came
  from for one day, only so one network can't make more than 20 accounts a day, and then delete
  it.

### Your profile

- **First and last name, gender (and whether to show it), a short intro, 3 to 5 interests, a
  profile photo and 4 to 6 more photos.** Other people see your first name and last initial,
  your photos, intro and interests, and your gender if you chose to show it, but only while
  they can see you in the app (see "Who sees what" below).
- **That you confirmed you are 18 or older.** Yap is for adults only.
- **Your account status** (active, hidden while reports are reviewed, or suspended) and when
  you joined.

### When you go live

- **Your area:** your phone sends its location, and we store only the centre of the square of
  about 100 metres it falls in, never the exact point (plus how accurate your phone says the
  location is, between 0 and 50 metres). We also keep the **note** you add ("How to find me")
  and when your session started and ends. We use the area only to work out who is in the same
  square as you or one next to it. It is **never shown to anyone**, and your live session is
  **deleted when you get off or your time runs out** (within a minute). The area is also kept as
  your last known area (see "Events on the map").
- **The times you started a session or moved** (not where), kept for 10 minutes so the app can
  limit how often a live location can change. This stops people from hopping around to find
  out who is live where.

### Saying hi and chatting

- **Who said hi to whom, when, and the answer** (accepted, declined, expired, ended, met).
- **Your messages**, with your display name and the time. Messages are only visible to the
  people in that chat.

These are kept until either person deletes their account.

### Events on the map

- **Events you create:** the title, description, category, start time and **the place you
  chose on the map**. The place is meant to be seen by others: that's how people find the
  event. It is not your own location.
- **Who joined each event** and **the group chat messages**.

Events and their group chats are kept until the host deletes the event or their account. The
messages you sent in other people's group chats are deleted when you delete your account.
When you use the map, your phone sends its location and we store your **area** (the centre of
the square of about 100 metres it falls in). The map lists events around your newest area, from
the map or from going live, for 2 hours after it was set, and joining an event checks you are
within 5 km of it. We keep only your **last known area** and when it was set, until a newer one
replaces it or you delete your account, so a new location far from it is refused unless you
could have travelled there since (5 km plus 1,000 km an hour). Like a live location, it can only
change a few times in 10 minutes, and it is never shown to anyone.

### Safety

- **People you block**, so you never see each other.
- **Reports you make** (who, the reason, any details you add, and a copy of the profile, message or event you
  reported, as it was then. A report made from a say-hi or chat also keeps a copy of up to the
  last 20 messages the reported person sent in it) and reports made about you. We
  read reports to keep Yap safe (see our [Community Guidelines](https://yap.samarxkx.dev/guidelines)). A profile is hidden until we
  have reviewed it when three different people report it who really interacted with that person
  on Yap (a chat between them, even one that has ended, a say-hi from that person, or a message
  that person posted in a group chat they are both in) or have used Yap for at least 3 days.
- **Banned email addresses.** If we suspend an account, or remove one for breaking the rules, we
  keep a scrambled (hashed) copy of its email address so it can't sign up again. We can also ban an
  address by hand. When an account is deleted, only a ban from a removal or one we added by hand
  is kept, until we lift it. Deleting your own account removes a ban from your own suspension.
- **Text checks.** Intros, interests, notes, event text and messages are checked against a
  short list of slurs and abusive words when you save or send them. Text that fails is
  refused, not stored.

### Beta testing

- **Your tester invitation.** To invite you, we add the email address you give us to Apple's
  TestFlight or to a Google Play tester list. It stays there until we remove it. We remove it
  within 30 days when you delete your account, leave the beta and tell us, or ask us.
- **Crash reports and feedback.** If your phone's settings allow it, Apple and Google share
  crash reports and app performance data with us, with your device model and system version.
  Feedback and screenshots you choose to send through TestFlight also reach us, with your
  device details. We use them only to fix problems.

### Technical information

- Our hosting provider keeps **short-lived logs of requests** to our servers, which include
  your IP address, the time and the app's version, to keep the service secure and fix
  problems. We don't use them to profile you.
- **Google Maps on Android.** The map on Android is drawn by Google's Maps SDK, which
  sends Google your device's IP address, crash reports and stack traces, device details (such
  as the model and system version), a pseudonymous identifier the SDK creates to count its daily
  users and, depending on how the map is used, map interactions such as panning and zooming.
  Google uses this to run and improve its services, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google lists what it collects
  in its [Maps SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/android-sdk/play-data-disclosure). On iPhone the map is Apple Maps, run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
- **On your phone**, the app stores your sign-in session, a few settings (such as the map
  distance you chose and how many times you've seen the chat safety tip) and a cache of photos. These are
  removed when you sign out or delete the app.

### What we don't collect

No contacts, no advertising identifiers, no tracking, no background location, no microphone,
and no payment details. Yap has no ads, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or crash
reporting tools of its own. Google Maps on Android collects the data described above.

## Who sees what

- **People nearby who are live at the same time** see your profile card and note. They never
  see your location on a map or how far away you are, but seeing you there tells them you are
  close, and your note can say where to find you.
- **Someone you said hi to, or who said hi to you**, sees your profile while the request is
  waiting and during and after a chat.
- **People who can see an event you host** (on the map within 50 km of their own area) see
  your first name and last initial. Only **people who joined the event** see your profile photo.
- **Nobody** sees you while either of you has blocked the other.

## Who helps us run Yap

We use these services to run the app. Supabase and Resend only process your data on our behalf,
under their own security and privacy terms, and we don't let them use it for anything else.
Apple and Google run their maps, app stores and beta testing services under their own privacy
policies.

| Service | What it does | What it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Database, sign-in, photo storage and live updates | Everything listed above |
| **Resend** | Sends our emails | Your email address and the code in the email |
| **Expo** (650 Industries) | Builds the app and delivers it to the stores | No personal data about you |
| **Apple Maps** (iPhone) / **Google Maps** (Android) | Draws the map | The map area you look at and your device's IP address. On Android, Google's Maps SDK also collects crash data, device details, an SDK identifier and map interactions (see "Technical information"). Both under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies |
| **Apple App Store and TestFlight / Google Play** | Delivers the app and its beta tests, and updates it | Your tester email address, crash reports and any feedback you send, under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies |

Our Supabase project is hosted in AWS ca-central-1, Canada (Central). Your data may be processed in other countries where these providers operate.

We **don't sell or rent your data**. Apart from what Apple and Google collect through the map,
the stores and TestFlight, as described above, we don't share it with anyone else unless the law
requires it (for example, a valid order from a court or the police) or it is needed to protect
someone's safety.

| Location-change times | 10 minutes |
| Hashed IP addresses of sign-ups | 1 day |

| Profile, photos, chats, messages, events, blocks | You delete your account (or we remove it for breaking the rules) |

| Server request logs | Removed automatically by our hosting provider after a few days |
| Tester email address (beta) | Until we remove it, within 30 days of you deleting your account, leaving the beta and telling us, or asking us |

| Backups | Daily backups kept for 7 days on the Supabase Pro plan |

## Deleting your account

In the app: **Profile → Settings (gear) → Delete account**, then confirm the deletion. This
deletes, straight away and whether or not your account is on hold:

- your account, email address and password
- your profile and every photo you uploaded, including old and replaced ones
- your live session, your last known area and the times you went live or moved
- every say-hi request and 1:1 chat you were part of, with all their messages, for both of you
- the events you host, with their members and group chats
- your event memberships and every message you sent in other people's group chats
- the people you blocked, and blocks other people made against you
- any ban on your email address from a suspension (a ban from an account we removed for breaking
  the rules, or one we added by hand, stays)

Only safety reports you made, or that were made about you, are kept, without your account
linked to them, so we can still act on them. Removing account links does not make report
details anonymous: details, and the copy of the reported profile, message or event a report
keeps (with up to the last 20 messages the reported person sent, for a report made from a
say-hi or chat), may still identify you or someone else. Nothing else is kept in Yap. Your beta tester invitation is separate
from your account: your email address stays in TestFlight or Google Play testing until we remove
it. We remove it within 30 days when you delete your account, leave the beta and tell us, or ask us.
The records of when you signed in are removed within 7 days, for everyone. Copies in backups
expire within 7 days.

If you can't use the app, email **support@samarxkx.dev** from the address you signed up with
and we'll delete your account within 30 days. The steps are also at
[yap.samarxkx.dev/delete-account](https://yap.samarxkx.dev/delete-account).

## Your choices and rights

- **See or correct your data:** your profile is in the app, and you can edit it any time.
  For a copy of everything we hold about you, email us.
- **Location:** Yap only asks for your location while the app is open. You can turn it off in
  your phone's settings at any time; you just won't be able to go live or see the map.
- **Photos and camera:** used only when you choose a photo or take one. Yap does not ask for selfies, does not compare faces and does not verify who you are.
- **Delete:** as described above.

Depending on where you live (for example in Canada, the EU or the UK, or California), you may
have rights to access, correct, delete, or move your data, or to object to how it is used.
Email us to use any of them; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data
protection authority.

## Adults only

Yap is only for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly keep data about anyone younger. If
we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it. If you think a child is
using Yap, report them in the app or email us.

## Security

Every connection to Yap is encrypted (HTTPS). Database rules let each person read only what
they are allowed to see, every change goes through checks on our server, and photos are
stored privately and shown through links that expire within 15 minutes. No system is perfect, so
please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you
in the app before they take effect.
