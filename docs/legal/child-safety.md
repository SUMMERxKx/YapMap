# Yap Child Safety Standards

**Last updated:** 18 November 2026

Yap is for adults aged **18 and over**. Children are not permitted to use Yap.

## What is prohibited

Yap prohibits **child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)** and **child sexual abuse
material (CSAM)**. This includes grooming, sexual solicitation of children, sexual
extortion, and creating, posting, sharing or seeking CSAM. These prohibitions apply to
profiles, photos, notes, events and messages.

## How to report a concern

Use the in-app report feature: tap **···** on a profile or in a chat, or long-press a
message in a group chat, and choose **Report**. Report accounts that appear to belong to
someone under 18 as well as suspected CSAE or CSAM.

You can also email **support@samarxkx.dev**, including if you cannot access the app.
Describe the concern and identify the account or content so we can locate it. Do not
attach or forward suspected CSAM.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In Canada, concerns
about online child sexual exploitation can also be reported to [Cybertip.ca](https://www.cybertip.ca/).

## What Yap does

We review every report within 24 hours, as set out in our
[Terms of Service](https://yap.samarxkx.dev/terms). We remove CSAE and CSAM content and
suspend or remove accounts that break these standards. If we learn that an account belongs
to someone under 18, we delete it.

We report child sexual abuse and exploitation material to the appropriate authorities as
legally required, including Cybertip.ca and law enforcement in Canada. Safety records may
be retained as described in our [Privacy Policy](https://yap.samarxkx.dev/privacy) and as
legally required.
